Logitech Wheel Test Wizard

Guided force-feedback tests for the Logitech G923, RS50 and G Pro. The wizard runs a few short, low-force tests and creates a report you can send back.

Step 1 Welcome

Before you start

Safety: the wheel will move on its own during the tests. Keep hands, cables and objects clear of the wheel, but stay ready to press Stop force now. Start with the wheel free to turn.

Close Logitech G HUB before connecting, because it can take control of the wheel.

For an RS50 or G Pro, use the mode you normally race in. Native mode and G923 compatibility mode are both supported.
