Logitech HID++ Test Bench

Standalone bring-up page. It talks directly to the wheel's HID++ interface, not through WheelManager. Use it to compare autostart, update, stop and resume strategies under sustained streaming. Driver: logitech-test.

How to use this test bench

1. Connect safely

Close Logitech G HUB first; it can claim the wheel. Click Connect Logitech wheel, choose the wheel in the browser picker, then Initialize HID++.

For split-interface wheels (RS50, G Pro) the page opens the joystick and HID++ interfaces. For G923 the input and HID++ collections share one interface.

Start with a low force and strength. Keep the wheel free of cables, hands and objects while streaming.

2. Compare strategies

Use the presets to reproduce known paths, or set the flags manually. The key question is whether the effect keeps playing while force is updated.

  • autostart allocate starts the effect when the slot is created. RS50 and G923 need this.
  • autostart update restarts the effect on every update. This is the old Lapster behaviour and may prevent sustained force.
  • START=0 updates modify the existing effect without restarting it. This is the current RS50 strategy.
  • autostart resume plus play after resume restarts a stopped effect.
  • recreate on resume destroys and reallocates the slot if restarting fails.

3. Read the evidence

The state panel shows the discovered feature index, slot count, autocenter slot, active slot, playing flag, level, queue depth and frame counters. The log shows every HID++ request/response in hex.

Streams stop automatically after 10 seconds. The page also stops the effect when it is hidden or unloaded.

Copy the state and the HID++ log when reporting a result. See the FFB workaround notes for protocol details.
